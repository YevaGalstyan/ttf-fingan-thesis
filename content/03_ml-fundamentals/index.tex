\section{Machine Learning Fundamentals}\label{sec:ml-fundamentals}
This chapter provides the machine learning background for the remainder of the thesis. It begins with the terminology of neural networks, their training, and recurrent architectures with the long short-term memory (LSTM) cell (Sec.~\ref{sec:nn}). Afterwards, the generative adversarial network (GAN) is introduced, followed by its conditional, forecasting, and financial variants (Sec.~\ref{sec:gans}). Finally, the related work on GANs for financial time series, option markets, and energy markets is reviewed (Sec.~\ref{sec:relwork}).
