\subsection{Summary of the Setup}\label{sec:method-summary}
The daily TTF front-month return series was constructed from the futures data and split chronologically into training, validation, and test splits. The options data was joined into a panel and restricted to call options in the moneyness band of \num{0.5} to \num{2.0} over the test split. The ForGAN architecture and the reference Fin-GAN implementation were adopted, with the data loading adapted to the daily TTF returns and the missing backward propagation added to the ForGAN baseline training loop. Ten cost function branches were trained, including the ForGAN baseline, trained on cross-entropy alone, and nine Fin-GAN branches with the economics-driven terms. Each branch was trained under ten configurations and one input scaling run, with five seeds each, and evaluated on the distributional statistics of the generated returns. The selected model generates price paths by recursive rollout. From these price paths, call options are priced by Monte Carlo simulation and converted into implied volatilities. A GBM and two bootstrap modes serve as benchmarks. Chapter~\ref{sec:exp} reports the results.
